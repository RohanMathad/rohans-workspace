## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a calm, high-clarity personal wealth intelligence experience. By blending European editorial minimalism with tactile dashboard modularity, it removes the anxiety and friction commonly associated with financial management. 

### Emotional Persona & Aesthetic Pillars
- **Serene Precision:** Numbers, financial growth targets, and cashflows are framed within soothing pastel tinted micro-surfaces (Mint, Coral/Peach, and Periwinkle/Lavender) set against ultra-crisp negative space.
- **Architectural Curvature:** A distinct hierarchy of radiuses—grounded by spacious, nested 24px–32px cards and contrasted by razor-sharp pill (9999px) capsules for navigations, action chips, and interactive toggles.
- **Tactile Softness:** Drawing inspiration from Finexy's capsule headers, iBanko's ambient KPI containers, and Zarss's grouped metric cards, the interface conveys an airy, effortless modernism that feels tactile yet weightless.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around clean tonal separation across dual modes, enriched by light pastel KPI backdrops and high-contrast indicators.

### Light Mode Foundations
- **Canvas Base:** `#F6F7FB` (Soft, neutral-cool paper tone)
- **Card Surface:** `#FFFFFF` (Pristine optical white)
- **Surface Elevation Muted:** `#EEF0F6` (Inactive inputs, pill tracks, subtle dividers)

### Dark Mode Foundations
- **Canvas Base:** `#12131A` (Deep midnight obsidian)
- **Card Surface:** `#1C1E26` (Lifted slate charcoal)
- **Surface Elevation Muted:** `#252834` (Pill tracks, stroke borders, secondary toggles)

### Accent & Pastel Tiers
- **Mint Green (Income, Surplus, Positive Delta):** Light tint `#E8F8F0` with strong deep forest text/fill `#168B53`. In Dark Mode, surfaces adopt `#162822` with vibrant mint accents `#3DDC84`.
- **Coral / Peach (Expenses, Alerts, Discretionary Spend):** Light tint `#FDF0EA` with bold terracotta text/fill `#D4532B`. In Dark Mode, surfaces adopt `#2B1B18` with vibrant coral `#FF8566`.
- **Lavender / Blue (Investments, Transfers, Scheduled Plans):** Light tint `#EEF2FF` with deep royal accent `#3F51B5`. In Dark Mode, surfaces adopt `#1A1D2E` with electric periwinkle `#818CF8`.

### Text & Contrast Hierarchy
- **Heading & Primary Text:** Light `#12131A` | Dark `#FFFFFF`
- **Secondary & Caption Labels:** Light `#6C7280` | Dark `#9CA3AF`
- **Dividers & Outlines:** Light `rgba(18, 19, 26, 0.06)` | Dark `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies to achieve an approachable, high-end geometric personality. Its contemporary curves feel welcoming yet robust when displaying critical ledger metrics.

### Typographic Rules & Numerical Clarity
- **Tabular Figures:** Always apply OpenType tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to metric counters, balance breakdowns, and financial table cells to ensure flawless column alignment.
- **Negative Kerning on Totals:** All metrics over 24px utilize negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver modern, poster-like impact.
- **Micro Labels:** Tiny metadata labels (`label-sm` and `label-md`) use semi-bold or bold weights with slight positive letter spacing to maintain legibility when juxtaposed against pastel backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column responsive fluid grid system designed for panoramic dashboard density.

### Breakpoints & Layout Adapters
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) canvas margins. Side navigation occupies a persistent capsule sidebar or vertical pill island.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters. KPI metrics wrap to a 2x2 grid structure, while secondary panels stack beneath main charts.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 12px or 16px gutters and 16px (`1rem`) screen margins. Side navigation folds into a bottom-floating pill capsule bar.

### Spacing Harmony
Components use internal padding of 24px (`space-lg`) up to 32px (`space-xl`) to establish an open, editorial feel. Child components and inner pill capsules strictly maintain 8px (`space-xs`) or 12px (`space-sm`) gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated via clean tonal layering, ultra-diffused atmospheric shadows, and delicate perimeter ghost borders rather than heavy drop-shadows.

### Layering Architecture
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** `#F6F7FB` (Light) / `#12131A` (Dark).
- **Layer 1 (Modular Primary Containers):** `#FFFFFF` (Light) / `#1C1E26` (Dark), paired with an ambient shadow: `0px 8px 30px rgba(18, 19, 26, 0.03)` in Light Mode, and `0px 10px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)` in Dark Mode. Both modes feature a 1px ghost border (`rgba(0,0,0, 0.04)` light / `rgba(255,255,255, 0.06)` dark).
- **Layer 2 (Pastel Micro-Cards & Inner Sections):** Pastel tinted surfaces (`#E8F8F0`, `#FDF0EA`, `#EEF2FF`) float flush inside Layer 1 cards without drop shadows, using 1px matching tonal tint borders for structure.
- **Layer 3 (Floating Pill Capsules & Overlays):** Interactive elements such as pill navbars and popover selectors employ soft ambient elevation: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(18, 19, 26, 0.08)` (Light) or `0px 16px 40px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)` (Dark).

## Shapes

The interface embraces a dynamic two-tier radius hierarchy: broad structural frames softened with generous curves, paired with pill-shaped interactive elements.

### Curvature System
- **Pill Capsules (9999px / Full Radius):** Reserved for high-interaction objects, including the floating header navigation bar, search bars, category tags, filter pills, action buttons (`Transfer`, `Deposit`), and floating bottom bars.
- **Master Cards & Dashboard Panels:** Formed with `24px` (`1.5rem`) to `32px` (`2rem`) border-radius, imparting a friendly, tactile card-deck quality.
- **Inner Micro-Cards & KPI Boxes:** Use `16px` (`1rem`) to `20px` (`1.25rem`) rounded corners to fit inside parent containers.

## Components

### 1. Capsule Navigation Bar
- **Structure:** Floating, centered pill container (`border-radius: 9999px`) styled after Finexy navigation.
- **Style:** Light mode uses pure white with subtle border and `0 10px 25px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`. Dark mode uses `#1C1E26` with border `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`.
- **States:** Active route item displays as an inverted solid pill (`#12131A` background with `#FFFFFF` text in light mode; `#FFFFFF` background with `#12131A` text in dark mode). Inactive links sit quietly in muted gray with hover transitions.

### 2. Pastel KPI Cards
- **Structure:** Inspired by iBanko and Zarss dashboards; compact 16px–20px rounded cards housing metric name, sparkline, total volume, and status chip.
- **Colors:**
  - *Income:* `#E8F8F0` canvas, `#168B53` icon pill, green trend sparkline.
  - *Expense:* `#FDF0EA` canvas, `#D4532B` icon pill, coral trend sparkline.
  - *Investments/Savings:* `#EEF2FF` canvas, `#3F51B5` icon pill, periwinkle trend sparkline.
- **Typography:** Display metric in bold tabular numbers with accompanying pill tag indicating percentage delta.

### 3. Action Buttons & Micro-Capsules
- **Primary Buttons:** High-contrast full pill (`9999px`), `#12131A` fill in light mode with `#FFFFFF` label; hover shifts to subtle dark slate `#2A2C38`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Pill Buttons:** Crisp 1px border `rgba(18, 19, 26, 0.12)`, fully transparent interior, and icon-supported typography.
- **Compact Quick-Action Row:** Pill buttons placed side-by-side (`Transfer`, `Request`, `Send`) with inner circular icon adornments.

### 4. Progress Bars & Analytics Breakdown
- **Track:** 8px–12px height with complete pill rounding (`border-radius: 9999px`) on background `#EEF0F6` (Light) / `#252834` (Dark).
- **Fill Segments:** Segmented multi-color fills using the signature pastel-bright spectrum: Mint for budget preserved, Coral for spend, Lavender for recurring commitments.
- **Bar Charts:** Rounded tops (`border-radius: 8px 8px 0 0`) with two-tone split bars showcasing spend vs. budget thresholds.

### 5. Transaction List Items
- **Structure:** Horizontal layout within white / slate cards.
- **Icon Container:** 44px pill or smooth 14px rounded rectangle featuring subtle pastel backgrounds matching category taxonomy (food, travel, income).
- **Details:** Bold title, muted timestamp, and trailing right-aligned tabular currency value (green with a `+` prefix for inflows, charcoal/white for expenses).

### 6. Inputs & Search Fields
- **Container:** Complete capsule shape (`border-radius: 9999px`) with height of 44px–48px.
- **States:** Default border is transparent with a soft background fill (`#F0F2F7` light, `#252834` dark). Focus state reveals a crisp 1.5px ring of `#12131A` (Light) or `#818CF8` (Dark) with clean icon prefixing.
